# Business Problem & Stakeholder Context

## Background

A digital content library changes as new titles are added and the catalogue expands across formats, countries and genres. A headline title count gives only a partial view. Content strategy teams need to understand what is changing within the catalogue and where additions are coming from.

## Problem statement

The organisation needs a clear and interactive way to monitor catalogue growth, format composition, geographic contribution and genre distribution over time.

The project addresses the reporting problem: stakeholders should be able to explore these dimensions using a consistent set of definitions rather than relying on separate static reports.

## Stakeholders and their needs

| Stakeholder | Information need | Potential use |
|---|---|---|
| Content strategy team | Growth, genre mix and format trends | Explore catalogue composition |
| Content acquisition team | Country and genre representation | Identify areas for further sourcing research |
| Business operations | Consistent KPI reporting | Track changes over time |
| Senior management | High-level catalogue trends | Review the overall direction of the library |

## Business questions

1. How many titles are in the catalogue?
2. How many titles were added in each year?
3. How does the catalogue split between movies and series?
4. How has that format mix changed over time?
5. Which countries are represented most often?
6. Which genres are most common?
7. How do the results change when filtering by year, type, country or genre?

## Scope

**In scope:** title metadata, content type, rating, release year, date added, country and genre.

**Out of scope:** viewing behaviour, watch time, subscriber retention, revenue, licensing costs, marketing performance and causal explanations for catalogue changes.

## Business value

The dashboard is intended to make catalogue reporting easier to explore and discuss. It can help teams identify patterns that deserve further investigation, but it should not be used alone to make acquisition or investment decisions. Those decisions require audience demand, cost and performance data.

## Success criteria

A stakeholder should be able to:
- see the size and composition of the catalogue;
- compare additions across years;
- explore movie and series trends;
- filter by country and genre;
- understand the definitions behind the KPIs; and
- distinguish catalogue presence from audience popularity.
