# Key Findings & Interpretation

The original project README records three qualitative observations from the dashboard. The source dataset and Power BI file were not available in the connected repository review, so exact counts, percentages and year values have not been added here. This is intentional: numerical findings should be calculated from the actual data, not estimated from a screenshot.

## 1. Content additions increased in later years

The original dashboard summary describes an upward pattern in content additions in later years.

**Business interpretation:** catalogue additions were not evenly distributed across the timeline. A content strategy team may want to understand which years contributed most to the library and whether the pattern is consistent across formats.

**What to validate:** count distinct titles by Date Added year and compare the result with the dashboard's trend visual.

## 2. Series content grew faster than movies over time

The original dashboard summary indicates that series additions increased faster than movie additions over the period shown.

**Business interpretation:** the format composition of the library may have shifted. It is useful to review both absolute additions and each format's share of total additions; a faster growth rate does not automatically mean series outnumber movies in the full catalogue.

**What to validate:** compare distinct movie and series titles by Date Added year, then calculate year-over-year change using a clearly defined base year.

## 3. Content sourcing became more geographically diverse

The original dashboard summary describes a broader geographic contribution in later periods.

**Business interpretation:** the countries represented in the library may have expanded over time. This can help content teams explore catalogue representation, but it does not by itself demonstrate audience demand or content quality.

**What to validate:** count distinct countries represented by titles added in each year. Document how titles linked to multiple countries are counted.

## 4. Genre contribution provides another view of catalogue mix

The dashboard includes genre-level exploration. Genre counts can help stakeholders understand which types of content are represented most frequently.

**Business interpretation:** a genre's presence in the catalogue is not the same as its popularity with viewers. Viewership, completion and customer feedback data would be needed to assess audience preference.

## Business follow-up

1. Identify the years with the largest increases in title additions.
2. Compare movie and series additions using both counts and shares.
3. Review how country representation changes over time.
4. Explore genre mix by format and year.
5. Combine catalogue metadata with viewership and cost data before making acquisition or investment decisions.
